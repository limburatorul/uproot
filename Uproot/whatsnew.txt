# Every release's notes, newest first, shown by What's new (WhatsNewWindow.cs). Built into the exe.
# "## version | date | title", one entry per x.y with its patches; then the notes: "- " a point,
# a line in ** ** a heading, anything else a paragraph. A release with a picture of its own has
# WhatsNew\<x.y>.jpg (1920 x 340, made from the studio's banner template, the month it came out); the others
# show their month's picture, WhatsNew\m<MM>.jpg, which must exist for every month listed.

## 1.0 | 2026-10-23 | Uninstall without the wait
Uproot lists every app on your PC the moment it opens, finds one as you type, and removes it with what it left behind.
**A list that keeps up with you**
- Desktop programs and Store apps in one list, searched as you type. Windows' own Installed apps page sizes each app by opening every one of its files; on one PC that was **224,000 files** and a search that took over 10 seconds.
- Sizes are measured in the background from the folders' listings, Store apps' data included, so you see what each app really takes.
- Entries whose uninstaller is gone (a deleted folder, a drive that's no longer there) are marked, and can be taken off the list.
**Remove, then clean up**
- Tick several apps and remove them in one go. Uproot waits for each uninstaller to really finish, even the ones that hand off to a copy of themselves.
- Afterwards it shows the folders and registry keys left behind, named after the app and used by nothing else still installed. Folders go to the Recycle Bin, keys are backed up to a .reg file first.
**1.0.2: what's left of apps that are gone**
- Entries that aren't really there any more are sorted by why: **Drive not connected** (installed on a drive that's unplugged now), **Files deleted** (its folder or uninstaller was removed) and **No uninstaller**. Each has its own filter, shown only when something's in it.
- Tick as many as you like and **delete them from the registry** in one go: they leave Uproot and Windows' own Installed apps. Each entry is saved to a .reg file first, and folders named after them start unticked, since one may hold saves.
- Easier to read: larger text, lighter grey for the second lines, a calmer background and links in green.
- **1.0.3:** the list and the details sit on solid cards, so their text is drawn with ClearType and reads sharp instead of soft. The scrollbar has a handle you can see, an app without an icon leaves no empty square, and an entry that can't be uninstalled shows no Uninstall button at all.
**1.0.4: easier on the eyes**
- A calmer look all round: more room in each row, a green bar beside the app you've picked, and a quiet tile where an app has no icon of its own.
- The filters for what's wrong (**Drive not connected**, **Files deleted**, **No uninstaller**) are orange now, set apart from Desktop and Store, and the badges in the list match them.
- The details show the app's icon beside its name, and a missing drive or folder is explained in a soft orange box. With nothing picked, the panel says so plainly instead of sitting empty.
- Dialogs, the leftovers list, Settings and these notes sit on solid panels, so their text is as sharp as the list's. Settings are grouped into cards.
- What you type in the search box lines up with its hint, and an Uninstall button you can't press yet looks plainly off instead of faded green.
**1.0.5: a new icon**
- **A new icon**, brighter and easier to spot in the taskbar.
- What's new now shows a picture for each release.
**Also**
- **1.0.1:** Uproot no longer closes a second after it opens when one app's icon carries a broken colour profile. A picture that can't be read is simply left blank.
- Works with File Labs (open an app's folder), SpaceScan (see what's inside it) and Shelf (your games are marked).
